# SamePage product context

## Audience

Small service businesses that send agreements to private or professional clients. The initial company user is one administrator; recipients do not need an account.

## Situation

Both parties may read the same clause differently. The disagreement often becomes visible only after work starts, when clarification is slower, more emotional, and harder to prove.

## Main task

Before signing elsewhere, the company turns an agreement into a short comprehension check, reviews the questions, shares a private link, and sees which expectations still need alignment.

## Payer value

- Detect mismatched expectations before they become disputes.
- Reduce repeated explanation calls and onboarding back-and-forth.
- Keep a timestamped answer trail showing what was understood.
- Reuse strong question sets and learn which document language repeatedly causes confusion.

## Constraints

- Three to ten multiple-choice questions.
- Raw documents are processed transiently and never stored.
- The company reviews every generated question and intended answer.
- Recipients should never be shamed with a red “wrong” result.
- Shared links must be revocable and versioned in the real product.
- Paid plan: €19.99/month for 50 AI document analyses. Free plan: manual question authoring.

## Non-goals

Contract drafting or editing, legal advice, e-signing, quotes, invoices, pricing, project management, and change orders.

## PoC evidence boundary

This repository is a static interaction prototype. Authentication, persistence, document parsing, OpenAI calls, secure links, Stripe billing, and analytics are intentionally simulated.
