# Overview

SamePage should feel like a careful working document, not a generic SaaS dashboard. The interface earns trust through plain language, visible status, strong hierarchy, and restrained visual decisions.

# Colors

- `canvas`: `#F5F7FA` — quiet page background.
- `surface`: `#FFFFFF` — working surfaces and document areas.
- `ink`: `#172033` — primary text and strong rules.
- `muted`: `#647084` — secondary explanations and metadata.
- `action`: `#2146D0` — primary actions and selected controls.
- `action-soft`: `#E9EEFF` — selected or informational backgrounds.
- `alignment`: `#B45B18` — points that require discussion, never generic decoration.
- `alignment-soft`: `#FFF1E6` — mismatch background.
- `success`: `#18765A` — completed and aligned states.
- `rule`: `#DCE1E8` — dividers and control borders.

# Typography

Use self-hosted IBM Plex Sans Variable throughout. Distinction comes from size, weight, line-height, and measure rather than mixing decorative families. Headlines are compact and direct. Body copy stays between 50 and 70 characters per line.

# Layout

Marketing pages use generous vertical rhythm and demonstrate the product mechanic above the fold. Application pages use one main work column with a restrained navigation rail. Related content sits close; major phases have larger separation. Use dividers and whitespace before containers.

# Elevation & depth

Most surfaces are flat. Use a single soft shadow only for floating menus or the focused demo document. Never combine a wide shadow with a heavy border.

# Shapes

Controls use 8px radius. Large surfaces use 12px at most. Pills are reserved for compact statuses, not navigation or decoration.

# Components

- Primary buttons use `action`, a visible focus ring, and describe the outcome.
- Status chips use semantic color and sentence case.
- Question choices are full-width radio rows with a clear selected state.
- Tables use whitespace and row dividers, not nested cards.
- Alerts explain what happened and what the person can do next.

# Do

- Show a real misunderstanding and its resolution.
- Make the next action obvious on every surface.
- Preserve input after recoverable failures.
- Design loading, empty, revoked, completed, and narrow-screen states.

# Don’t

- No gradients, glow, glassmorphism, decorative grids, or AI sparkles.
- No badge above a giant hero.
- No interchangeable icon-card feature grid.
- No cards inside cards or decorative side borders.
- No useless KPI cards, excessive pills, or oversized icons.
- No generic “AI-powered clarity platform” language.
